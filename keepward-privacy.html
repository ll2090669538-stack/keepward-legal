Keepward Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026 · Keepward is called “来去” in Simplified Chinese.

Keepward is a local-first photo organizer. Your photos, videos, review history, and app settings stay on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this content.

Photo library access

With your permission, Keepward accesses the Apple Photos library to display, filter, favorite, organize, share, and delete items you explicitly select. Photo and video content is processed on your device and is not uploaded to the developer.

Information stored on your device

Review progress, cleanup records, quick-album choices, and app preferences are stored locally so the app can restore your experience. Removing the app deletes its local app data. Items in your system Photos library remain under your control in the Photos app.

Purchases

Keepward Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive your full payment-card or banking information. Apple may provide purchase status so the app can unlock or restore Pro features.

Notifications

If you enable memory reminders, Keepward schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder content is not sent to a developer server.

Sharing

Photos or videos leave the app only when you choose Share and select a destination in Apple’s system share sheet. The selected destination’s privacy policy then applies.

Data collection and tracking

Keepward has no account system, advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. We do not collect personal information, photo-library content, usage analytics, or diagnostics on a developer server.

Your controls

You can change Photos and notification permissions at any time in Apple Settings. You can also remove locally stored app data by deleting the app.

Children’s privacy

Keepward is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If Keepward’s data practices change, this page will be updated before the new practices take effect.

Keepward（来去）隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 20 日

Keepward（来去）是一款本地优先的照片整理工具。照片、视频、浏览记录和应用设置均保留在你的设备上，开发者不运营接收这些内容的服务器。

照片图库权限

经你授权后，Keepward 会访问 Apple 照片图库，用于展示、筛选、收藏、整理、分享，以及删除你明确选择的项目。照片与视频内容仅在设备上处理，不会上传给开发者。

保存在设备上的信息

浏览进度、清理记录、快捷相册和应用偏好会保存在本机，以便恢复使用状态。卸载应用会删除应用内本地数据；系统照片图库中的内容仍由你在“照片”App 中管理。

购买

Keepward Pro 购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。开发者不会取得你的完整银行卡或银行信息。Apple 可能向应用提供购买状态，用于解锁或恢复 Pro 权益。

通知

开启回忆提醒后，Keepward 只在设备上安排本地通知，提醒内容不会发送到开发者服务器。

分享

只有当你主动点击分享并在 Apple 系统分享面板中选择接收方时，所选照片或视频才会离开应用；之后适用接收方的隐私政策。

数据收集与跟踪

Keepward 不要求账号，不含广告、第三方分析或跨应用跟踪，也不会在开发者服务器收集个人信息、照片内容、使用分析或诊断数据。

你的控制

你可以随时在 Apple“设置”中更改照片和通知权限，也可以通过卸载应用删除应用内本地数据。

儿童隐私

Keepward 是面向普通用户的工具，不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

政策更新

如果 Keepward 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会在新方式生效前更新本页面。
